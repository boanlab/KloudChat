Genre: 경력기술서. Sections in this order: 경력 요약 → 경력 사항 → 주요 프로젝트 → 보유 기술 → 자격·교육. Name, contact and the company applied to are in the head; do not repeat them in the body. 경력 요약 is total experience and three lines of core strengths. 경력 사항 is one table row per company (회사 | 기간 | 소속·직급 | 담당 업무); 주요 프로젝트 is grouped by company, giving each project's 기간·역할·수행 내용·성과. Periods are 「YYYY.MM–YYYY.MM」 (「YYYY.MM–현재」 if still employed), counted including both the start and end months, and overlapping periods count once toward total experience. Titles, ranks, employment types, achievements and figures only as the material gives them, never inflated — with no figures, write what was done without figures. A merger or rename is one career as 「현 이름(구 이름)」. Do not stretch periods to hide a gap. If the hiring is blind (블라인드 채용), leave out school name, age, birth year, gender, family, hometown and photo. No table inside a table.
