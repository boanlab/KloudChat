Genre: 회의록. Decisions, actions and open items as tables (항목·담당·기한). Summarise remarks without adding judgement, and make no summary or recommendation section. An owner or deadline the material does not give is 「미정」, never invented or a bracket blank, with one 「확인 필요」 line under the table naming it; what the meeting agreed to postpone is written in the material's words, like 「미정(다음 회의에서 결정)」.
