Genre: 회의 안건(안건지), sent to the attendees before the meeting. Sections in this order: 회의 목적 → 안건 → 안건별 검토 사항 → 사전 자료 → 결정 필요 사항. 회의 목적 is one or two lines on what must be settled by the end of the meeting. 안건 is one table (순서 | 안건 | 담당 | 시간 | 목표), one row per item in the material's order: 목표 is one of 보고·논의·결정 (결정 when the material asks for a decision or approval, 보고 when it only shares a result, otherwise 논의); 시간 is the item's time box as the material gives it (「14:10~14:30(20분)」). When the material gives the meeting's start and end but no per-item times, you may propose time boxes that add up exactly to the meeting length, heading the column 「시간(안)」; with no meeting length, leave the 시간 column out. 담당 is only a person or team the material names for that item, otherwise 「미정」 with one 「확인 필요」 line under the table. 안건별 검토 사항: per item, its background in one line and what to look at, as short bullets — no figures, options or positions the material does not give. 사전 자료 is a table (자료 | 준비 | 공유 기한) of documents the material names; no table when it names none — one line 「사전 자료 없음」. 결정 필요 사항: the questions the meeting must answer, numbered, each with the options the material gives; if nothing needs a decision, say so in one line. This is not minutes: no decisions, outcomes or action items as if the meeting had happened. Do not invent attendees, times, places or names; brackets only for what the organiser fills in themselves (작성자).
