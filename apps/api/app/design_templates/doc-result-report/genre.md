Genre: 결과 보고서(행사·사업·교육·출장). Sections in this order: 개요 → 추진 경과 → 주요 결과·실적 → 성과 및 문제점 → 향후 계획(개선 사항). 추진 경과 holds only the steps and dates the material gives; when it gives no dated steps, leave that section out and keep its figures (신청·선발 수, 행사 일시) in 개요 — never fill it with dates the material does not have. 개요 is a Markdown table (`| 구분 | 내용 |`) with 일시·장소·참석·주관 one row each — a table, never list items like 「- 구분: 일시」. 개요 opens with a 「결과 요약」 of three or four items. 주요 결과 in figures (attendance, satisfaction, amount spent and spending rate) — against the plan, if there is one, as a table (항목 | 계획 | 실적 | 비율); spending as a table (항목 | 집행액 | 비율) whose rows sum exactly to the total spent, a 계획 column only when every item has a plan amount, then 「편성 ○○ / 집행 ○○ / 잔액 ○○(집행률 ○○%)」; stated totals copied, never re-added. Pair each problem with its improvement. Do not invent results or satisfaction the material does not give. Style: 개조식. No prose paragraphs. Write the content as Markdown lists and show the hierarchy by depth — level one `- `, level two indented two spaces `  - `, level three `    - `, level four `      - `. Do not write the □·○·-·ㆍ symbols yourself; typesetting draws them. End items in the noun form (개조식 명사형 종결: 「…함」 「…임」 「…예정」 「…필요」) — never in 「~합니다」. One or two lines per item; put units on numbers (「320명」 「1,250만 원」). A supplementary note goes on the line after its item as one line starting with 「※ 」. Notation: dates 「2026. 10. 7.」 (full stops instead of 연·월·일, no leading zeros), times 「14:00」 (24-hour clock), an amount for approval 「금113,560원(금일십일만삼천오백육십원)」. Attachments go at the end of the last section as one line 「붙임  1. ○○○ 1부.」, and from the next line 「2. ○○○ 1부.」 if there are more — with one attachment, no number: 「붙임  ○○○ 1부.」. Do not write 「끝.」 (typesetting adds it). Do not invent dates and times, places, head counts, amounts or names the material does not give, and do not leave bracket blanks for them either: write the line so it holds without the value (「장소: 확정 후 안내」), or 「미정」 in a table cell with one 「확인 필요」 line under the table; brackets only for what the person fills in themselves (작성자, 서명, 연락처).
