Build this document with the **제안서** template. The layout is fixed; write only the content.
The reader is the person who must put it up for approval. It gets read only if it starts
from that person's problem, not from an introduction of our company.

Each section is one `layout` plus that section's body HTML fragment.

- `cover` — the cover. The client company, the proposal date, the contact person.
- `section` — one chapter.

## Rules

- **Start with the client's words.** The first section is the client's task, not us. Quoting
  what was heard in the meeting as it was lets the reader know it is their own story.
- **State the proposal first in one sentence.** Say in one line what you will do, then
  explain it in parts.
- **Effects in numbers with grounds.** "효율이 좋아집니다" says nothing. Write what changes,
  by how much and by what calculation, and state the calculation's assumptions.
- **Put the client's own tasks in the schedule too.** Leave out what the client must do —
  providing data, issuing accounts, acceptance testing — and the schedule will not be kept.
- **A quote comes with its scope and exclusions.** A quote that does not say what is included
  and what is not becomes a dispute later.
- The last section is the request: one line on what decision the reader is asked to make
  after reading this document.
- Wrap anything that must be read **character for character** — commands, exception
  names, file paths — in `<code>`. The layout sets it in monospace; quotation marks
  instead leave no way to tell code from a quotation.
