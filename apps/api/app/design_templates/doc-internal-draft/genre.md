Genre: 간이기안문(내부결재, 행정업무운영 시행규칙 별지 제2호서식). Typesetting draws the production registration number, registration date, approval date, disclosure class and the approval box (기안자·검토자·결재권자), so do not write them in the body. The body is the one matter for approval — 관련 근거 → 목적·필요성 → 주요 내용(무엇을, 언제, 얼마에) → 행정 사항 → 붙임. An amount for approval carries its calculation grounds. Two or three sections are enough. Style: 개조식. No prose paragraphs. Write the content as Markdown lists and show the hierarchy by depth — level one `- `, level two indented two spaces `  - `, level three `    - `, level four `      - `. Do not write the □·○·-·ㆍ symbols yourself; typesetting draws them. End items in the noun form (개조식 명사형 종결: 「…함」 「…임」 「…예정」 「…필요」) — never in 「~합니다」. One or two lines per item; put units on numbers (「320명」 「1,250만 원」). A supplementary note goes on the line after its item as one line starting with 「※ 」. Notation: dates 「2026. 10. 7.」 (full stops instead of 연·월·일, no leading zeros), times 「14:00」 (24-hour clock), an amount for approval 「금113,560원(금일십일만삼천오백육십원)」. Attachments go at the end of the last section as one line 「붙임  1. ○○○ 1부.」, and from the next line 「2. ○○○ 1부.」 if there are more — with one attachment, no number: 「붙임  ○○○ 1부.」. Do not write 「끝.」 (typesetting adds it). Do not invent dates and times, places, head counts, amounts or names the material does not give; leave the place's name in brackets (「[일시]」 「[장소]」 「[금액]」).
