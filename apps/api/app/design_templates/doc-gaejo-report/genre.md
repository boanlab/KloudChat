Genre: 개조식 보고서(검토·현황·업무 보고). At the very start of the first section put the two key lines in a callout block, opened by the line 「```callout」 (not a bare 「```」) — first line 「핵심」 alone, then the conclusion and the decision needed (or the next action) on one line each — plain lines inside the block, not list items (the list rule below is for the body). Sections in this order: 현황 → 문제점 및 검토 → 향후 조치 및 결정 요청 → 붙임. Keep detailed tables, alternative comparisons and calculation grounds out of the body; put them in the 「붙임」 section. The 붙임 list names only those tables or attachments the material names — never one you invent; with none, the 붙임 section is the one line 「붙임 없음」. Style: 개조식. No prose paragraphs. Write the content as Markdown lists and show the hierarchy by depth — level one `- `, level two indented two spaces `  - `, level three `    - `, level four `      - `. Do not write the □·○·-·ㆍ symbols yourself; typesetting draws them. End items in the noun form (개조식 명사형 종결: 「…함」 「…임」 「…예정」 「…필요」) — never in 「~합니다」. One or two lines per item; put units on numbers (「320명」 「1,250만 원」). A supplementary note goes on the line after its item as one line starting with 「※ 」. Notation: dates 「2026. 10. 7.」 (full stops instead of 연·월·일, no leading zeros), times 「14:00」 (24-hour clock), an amount for approval 「금113,560원(금일십일만삼천오백육십원)」. Attachments go at the end of the last section as one line 「붙임  1. ○○○ 1부.」, and from the next line 「2. ○○○ 1부.」 if there are more — with one attachment, no number: 「붙임  ○○○ 1부.」. Do not write 「끝.」 (typesetting adds it). Do not invent dates and times, places, head counts, amounts or names the material does not give; leave the place's name in brackets (「[일시]」 「[장소]」 「[금액]」). Issues, risks and decisions are only the ones the material raises — never a VAT basis or other question the material does not mention — and each item stays under its own subject.
