Genre: 추진 계획(안)(행사·사업·교육). Sections in this order: 추진 배경 및 목적 → 추진 개요 → 세부 추진 내용 → 소요 예산 → 추진 일정 → 기대 효과. 추진 개요 is a table (구분 | 내용) with 일시·장소·대상·인원·주관 one row each. 소요 예산 is a table (항목 | 산출 근거 | 금액); 산출 근거 is 「단가 × 수량」 and the last row is the total — the total must equal the sum of the item amounts. 추진 일정 is a table (일정 | 내용 | 담당). 기대 효과 without guessed figures. Attachments, if any, go at the end of the 기대 효과 section. Style: 개조식. No prose paragraphs. Write the content as Markdown lists and show the hierarchy by depth — level one `- `, level two indented two spaces `  - `, level three `    - `, level four `      - `. Do not write the □·○·-·ㆍ symbols yourself; typesetting draws them. End items in the noun form (개조식 명사형 종결: 「…함」 「…임」 「…예정」 「…필요」) — never in 「~합니다」. One or two lines per item; put units on numbers (「320명」 「1,250만 원」). A supplementary note goes on the line after its item as one line starting with 「※ 」. Notation: dates 「2026. 10. 7.」 (full stops instead of 연·월·일, no leading zeros), times 「14:00」 (24-hour clock), an amount for approval 「금113,560원(금일십일만삼천오백육십원)」. Attachments go at the end of the last section as one line 「붙임  1. ○○○ 1부.」, and from the next line 「2. ○○○ 1부.」 if there are more — with one attachment, no number: 「붙임  ○○○ 1부.」. Do not write 「끝.」 (typesetting adds it). Do not invent dates and times, places, head counts, amounts or names the material does not give, and do not leave bracket blanks for them either: write the line so it holds without the value (「장소: 확정 후 안내」), or 「미정」 in a table cell with one 「확인 필요」 line under the table; brackets only for what the person fills in themselves (작성자, 서명, 연락처).
