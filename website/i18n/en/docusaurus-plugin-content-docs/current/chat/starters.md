# Templates and designs

These two features have similar names, but each has a different role and scope. Work you do often belongs to [agents](agents.md) and workflows.

| Feature | Role | Where to select it | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Template** | The form of the output. The product includes 8 report templates and 7 slide templates. | **+** → Pick a template in the composer, and Customize → Templates tab | That conversation. Clear it with × on the chip |
| **Design** | A setting that bundles colors, typefaces, and writing style. You link it to a project. | Customize → Designs tab | Every output in that project |

## Templates

A template defines the structure rules and the typesetting of reports and slides. Select one and the **What this template checks** list appears. You edit a document in a template by rewriting it one block at a time, and you can export it as PDF, DOCX/PPTX, HWPX, HTML, or text. Templates are built into the product, so you cannot create new ones. Images, audio and video have no templates.

## Designs

Manage them in **Customize** → the **Designs** tab. Designs other people shared appear under **Explore** on the same tab.

![The design screen](/img/guide/designs.png)

- Set the name, the accent color, the body color, the secondary color, the typeface (sans-serif or serif), the writing style rules, the image style, and the rules to apply alongside them.
- **Import from a document** extracts a draft from an existing hwpx, docx, or pdf file, or from a web address. It calls the model once, and you can review the content before saving.
- The writing style applies to conversations, reports, and slides. Colors and typefaces apply to slides and report covers. The image style applies to images. Nothing applies to audio and video.
- Design settings are copied into an output when it is generated. Changing the design later does not change outputs that already exist.
- An administrator can distribute a read-only shared design with **Provide to all users**.
