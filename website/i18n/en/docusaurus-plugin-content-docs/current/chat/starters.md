# Templates and themes

These two features have similar names, but each has a different role and scope (they used to be called 양식 and 디자인). Work you do often belongs to [agents](agents.md). Neither is a Customize tab: you pick each where it is used.

| Feature | Role | Where to pick it | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Template** | The form of the output. The product includes report and slide templates. | **+** → **Pick a template** in the composer; **Output** › **Template** in the agent settings | That conversation (clear it with × on the chip), or the conversations that agent opens |
| **Theme** | A setting that bundles colors, typefaces, and writing style. | **Output** › **Theme** in the agent settings, **Edit › Theme** in the report or slides panel, the project settings | That agent's or project's outputs |

## Templates

A template defines the structure rules and the typesetting of reports and slides. Select one and the **What this template checks** list appears. You edit a document in a template by rewriting it one block at a time, and you can export it as PDF, DOCX/PPTX, HWPX, HTML, or text. Templates are built into the product, so you cannot create new ones. Images, audio and video have no templates. Retired templates (career history, client proposal) can no longer be picked, but documents made with them still open and export.

The built-in templates in the picker are six documents (internal draft 간이기안문, plan 추진 계획(안), result report 결과 보고서, bullet-style briefing 개조식 보고서, comparison report 비교·분석 보고서, minutes 회의록) and the **Briefing** deck. They write straight into the institution's format and show that format's check results when done. The other deck templates are hidden from the picker, not deleted: conversations and agents that used one keep writing and exporting with it.

## Themes

Manage them in **Settings** → **Themes** (also opened by **Manage themes** in the agent settings). Themes other people shared appear under **Shared themes** on the same tab.

![The theme screen](/img/guide/designs.png)

- Set the name, the accent color, the body color, the secondary color, the typeface (sans-serif or serif), the writing style rules, the image style, and the rules to apply alongside them.
- **Import from a document** extracts a draft from an existing hwpx, docx, or pdf file, or from a web address. It calls the model once, and you can review the content before saving.
- The writing style applies to conversations, reports, and slides. Colors and typefaces apply to slides and report covers. The image style applies to images. Nothing applies to audio and video.
- Theme settings are copied into an output when it is generated. Changing the theme later does not change outputs that already exist.
- An administrator can distribute a read-only shared theme with **Provide to all users**.
- Of the built-in themes, the pickers show **Default** (기본) and **Document serif** (문서용 명조). **Presentation teal** (발표용 청록) is no longer given to new accounts. Those who already have it keep it as their own theme, and agents and outputs that wear it keep it.
