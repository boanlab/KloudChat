# HWP documents

Open a Hangul (HWP) editor beside the conversation and write or edit an HWP document, such as an official letter, by chat. You can also edit the same document directly in the editor.

## Getting started

- On Home or in an empty report conversation, choose **Report → Format → HWP** and press **Start blank** to start a conversation with a blank document open. Send a topic to have the HWP document written. The conversation is on the left; on the right is the HWP editor with its menus (File, Edit, View, Insert, Format, Page, Table, Tools) and toolbar.
- A `.hwp` or `.hwpx` file attached in the conversation opens in the editor with **Open as HWP**. A `.hwp` file is saved as HWPX the first time it opens.
- Asking in chat for "an HWP document" or "an hwp file" also creates one.

## Writing and editing by chat

- Ask, for example, "Write an official letter asking staff to attend a training", and a document in the official-letter layout (recipient/sender/date table, title, numbered items, 끝.) appears in the editor.
- Facts you did not give, such as the organisation, dates or place, are not invented: they stay as bracket blanks like `[기관명]` or `[YYYY. MM. DD.]`.
- When you give the facts ("the training is 교원연수, on 11 Feb 2027 10:00-12:00, …"), the agent reads the document and fills those blanks and lines. Paragraph and character formatting stay as they were.
- The steps above the answer show how often the document was read and edited, for example "Check document 2 calls · Edit document 2 calls".
- While the agent edits, the editor is locked; when it saves a version, the editor loads it.

## Editing by hand and saving

- After an edit in the editor, the header shows **Not saved**. **Save** stores it as a new version.
- If you send a message with unsaved edits, you first choose **Save and send**, **Discard and send** or **Cancel**. The agent always starts from the saved document.
- If another version was saved in the meantime, your save is refused and you are told so. Choose **Download my edits** to keep your edits as a file, or **Load the latest version**.
- Versions are listed and restored under ⋯ → **Version history**.

## Downloads and limits

- **Download** offers HWPX or HWP (Hangul 97–2022).
- The editor is served from this server; documents are not sent anywhere else. Open fonts stand in for the Hamchorom fonts, so line breaks can differ from Hancom Office.
- The agent can change body paragraphs and table cells and rows. Pictures, text boxes, headers and footers, and adding or removing rows in tables with merged cells are edited in the editor.
- At phone width the document can be read and downloaded; save on a wider screen.
