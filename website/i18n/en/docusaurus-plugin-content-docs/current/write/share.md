# Sharing

Create a link with the **Share** button at the top right of the conversation screen.

![Share settings window](/img/guide/share-dialog.png)

## Creating a link

1. Choose who can see it.

   | Scope | Who can view it |
   |---|---|
   | People with an account | Every user signed in to this service. Restrictions by department or team do not apply. |
   | People with the link | Anyone who knows the link, and they can view it without an account. |

2. Press **Create the link** and then **Copy**.

After creation, the status is shown as **Account required** or **Opens without an account**.

## What is shared

- The conversation and the outputs generated in it are made public. The output is shown first, and the conversation follows. For some types, a message says that the share screen does not support them yet.
- **Project files and memory are not shared.** Files attached to the conversation are exposed as far as they are quoted in the answer.
- **Conversation that continues after sharing keeps appearing in the link as well.** To make only part of it public up to a certain point, revoke the link and create a new one.

## View history and revocation

In the same window you can check the viewer (name and email, or "visitor without an account"), the browser, the region, and the number of views. Repeat views within one hour are counted as one. When you run **Revoke the link**, that address stops opening immediately. The link is also blocked if you delete the conversation.
