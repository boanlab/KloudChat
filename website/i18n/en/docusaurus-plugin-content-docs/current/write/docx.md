# Word documents

Open a Word editor beside the conversation and write or edit a Word document (`.docx`), such as an official letter or a report, by chat. You can also edit the same document directly in the editor.

## Getting started

- On Home, **Word 문서** (Word document) starts a conversation with a blank document open. The conversation is on the left; on the right is the editor with a Word-style ribbon (File, Home, Insert, Layout, References, Review and View tabs). Font, size, bold, colour, alignment, lists and indents are on **Home**; tables are on **Insert**. The ribbon and its menus are in the app's language (Korean or English).
- The font list on **Home** starts with Korean fonts: Malgun Gothic (맑은 고딕), Nanum Gothic, Nanum Myeongjo, Batang, Dotum, Gulim, Gungsuh, HCR Batang, HCR Dotum and Pretendard. Choosing a Korean font saves it as the text's Korean font, as Word does, so Word and Hancom Office show it too; choosing a Latin font applies to Latin text and keeps the Korean font.
- A `.docx` file attached in the conversation opens in the editor with **Open in Word editor**.
- Asking in chat for something "in Word", "as a Word document" or "as a docx file" writes it in this conversation's Word document instead of moving to the report screen.
- Legacy `.doc` files cannot be opened. Save them as `.docx` in Word and attach that.

## Writing and editing by chat

- Ask, for example, "Write an official letter in Word asking staff to attend a training", and a document in the official-letter layout (recipient/sender/date table, title, numbered items, 끝.) appears in the editor.
- Facts you did not give, such as the organisation, dates or place, are not invented: they stay as bracket blanks like `[기관명]` or `[YYYY. MM. DD.]`.
- When you give the facts, the agent reads the document and fills those blanks and lines. Paragraph styles, numbering, character formatting and table cell formatting stay as they were. A blank is found even when Word stored the line in several pieces.
- Blanks in headers and footers are filled too; fields such as page numbers are left as they are.
- The steps above the answer show how often the document was read and edited, for example "Check document 2 calls · Edit document 2 calls".
- While the agent edits, the editor is locked; when it saves a version, the editor loads it.

## Editing by hand and saving

- After an edit in the editor, the header shows **Not saved**. **Save**, or Ctrl+S in the editor, stores it as a new version.
- If you send a message with unsaved edits, you first choose **Save and send**, **Discard and send** or **Cancel**. The agent always starts from the saved document.
- If another version was saved in the meantime, your save is refused and you are told so. Choose **Download my edits** to keep your edits as a file, or **Load the latest version**.
- Versions are listed and restored under ⋯ → **Version history**.

## Downloads and limits

- **Download** → **Word document** saves what the editor shows as a `.docx` file. For a PDF, **File → Export** (or **Print**) on the ribbon opens the print dialog; choose **Save as PDF** there.
- The editor runs inside this site; documents are not sent anywhere else. Calibri, Arial, Times New Roman and similar fonts are shown in open fonts of the same widths. Korean text is shown in the document's Korean font; when your computer lacks it, serif fonts such as Batang and Gungsuh are shown in Nanum Myeongjo and sans fonts such as Malgun Gothic, Gulim and Dotum in Nanum Gothic. A stand-in's letters are slightly different in width, so line breaks can differ from Word.
- A Korean document breaks lines between words (at spaces), as Word does, and puts a little space between Hangul and Latin letters or digits. Page breaks and printing follow the same lines.
- The agent can change body paragraphs, table cells and rows, and blanks in headers and footers. Pictures, text boxes, and adding or removing rows in tables with vertically merged cells are edited in the editor.
- At phone width the document can be read and downloaded; edit and save on a wider screen.
