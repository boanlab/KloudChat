# PowerPoint documents

Open a PowerPoint editor beside the conversation and fill or edit a PowerPoint file (`.pptx`), such as a deck you received or your company's template, by chat. You can also edit the same file directly in the editor.

A deck KloudChat writes from scratch is made on the [Slides](slides) screen by default, and it downloads as PPTX too. This page is for when the PowerPoint file itself has to be worked on.

## Getting started

- On Home, **PowerPoint** starts a conversation with a blank deck open. The conversation is on the left; on the right is an editor in the manner of Google Slides: a toolbar with undo, zoom, text box, shape, image, table, font, size, bold, italic, underline, text colour, alignment and bullets, the slide list on the left and the speaker notes below.
- A `.pptx` file attached in the conversation opens in the editor with **Open in PowerPoint editor**.
- Asking in chat for something "in PowerPoint", "as a PowerPoint file", "as a pptx file" or "as a PPT file" writes it in this conversation's PowerPoint document instead of moving to the slides screen. A request that names no file format, such as "write a presentation" or "write a PPT", is made on the slides screen.
- Legacy `.ppt` files cannot be opened. Save them as `.pptx` in PowerPoint and attach that.

## Writing and editing by chat

- Ask, for example, "Make a five-slide business plan report in PowerPoint", and a deck with a cover and a slide per item (bullets or a table) appears in the editor.
- Facts you did not give, such as the department, dates, figures or who is responsible, are not invented: they stay as bracket blanks like `[담당자]` or `[YYYY. MM. DD.]`.
- When you give the facts, the agent reads the file and fills those blanks and lines, table cells and speaker notes. Bullets, levels, character formatting and cell colours stay as they were; only what changed is rewritten.
- You can add, duplicate, move and delete slides: "add a schedule slide after slide 3", "duplicate slide 2", "delete the last slide". A slide added to a company template or a received file follows that file's layouts and look.
- While the agent edits, the editor is locked; when it saves a version, the editor loads it. The cursor stays in the chat box you were typing in.

## Editing by hand and saving

- Double-click text on a slide to edit it. Select characters and change bold, size or colour, or press Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I or Ctrl+U. With only a caret, a format applies to the word it stands in.
- Right-click a shape or text box for Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Bring forward, Send backward and Delete. Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+D, Delete and Ctrl+Z work too.
- Drag slides in the slide list to reorder them; the ⋮ menu or a right-click gives New slide, Duplicate, Move up, Move down and Delete.
- After an edit, the header shows **Not saved**. **Save**, or Ctrl+S in the editor, stores it as a new version.
- If you send a message with unsaved edits, you first choose **Save and send**, **Discard and send** or **Cancel**. The agent always starts from the saved file.
- If another version was saved in the meantime, your save is refused and you are told so. Choose **Download my edits** to keep your edits as a file, or **Load the latest version**.
- Versions are listed and restored under ⋯ → **Version history**.

## Downloads and limits

- **Download** → **PowerPoint document** saves what the editor shows as a `.pptx` file.
- The editor runs inside this site; files are not sent anywhere else. Fonts such as Malgun Gothic are shown in the fonts installed on your computer, so line breaks can differ slightly from PowerPoint. Fonts embedded in the MTX format in old files are not unpacked and are shown in another font.
- The agent can change the text of text boxes and placeholders, table cells and rows, and speaker notes, and add, duplicate, move and delete slides. Charts, pictures and SmartArt are edited in the editor.
- At phone width the file can be read and downloaded; edit and save on a wider screen.
