# Signing up and signing in

This page explains how to request an account and sign in, and how to manage your password and your devices.

![Sign-in screen](/img/guide/login.png)

## Requesting an account

1. On the sign-in screen, select the **Sign up** tab.
2. Enter your name, email and password, then press **Request sign-up**. The password must be at least 10 characters long and contain a number and a symbol. If the screen lists the addresses that may sign up, only email at those domains can apply.
3. What happens next depends on the sign-up method the administrator has set.

| Sign-up method | Right after you apply |
|---|---|
| Use after approval | A waiting screen appears. It checks the status every 15 seconds and moves to the home screen automatically once you are approved. |
| Use immediately | You can start using the service right away, with no waiting. |

If pressing **Request sign-up** shows the notice "Sign-ups are not being accepted at the moment", ask your administrator to create an account for you. Where email verification is configured, a confirmation mail is sent. If it does not arrive, you can send it again after one minute.

## Signing in

Sign in with your email and password. **If you enter the wrong password five times in a row, sign-in is blocked for 15 minutes.** While it is blocked, even the correct password will not sign you in, so try again after 15 minutes.

A suspended account sees an information screen instead of signing in. In that case, contact your administrator.

## Resetting your password

If the sign-in screen has a **Forgot your password?** link, you can receive a reset mail. The link in the mail is valid for 30 minutes and can be used only once. When you set a new password you are signed in immediately and **every session on your other devices is signed out.** If the link is not shown, no mail server is configured in your environment, so ask your administrator to reset the password for you.

## Changing your password and managing devices

- Changing your password: Account menu → **Settings** → **Profile**. Changing it signs you out on your other devices.
- List of signed-in devices and sign-in history: **Settings** → **Security**. For details, see [Settings](../personal/settings#security).

## Automatic sign-out

Where the administrator has set an idle time, you are signed out automatically after that time without any activity. When you connect again, the sign-in screen shows a notice that you were signed out automatically.

## Interface language

Use the **EN** button at the top right to switch the interface language. The language of an answer follows **the language you asked in**, whatever the interface language is.
