# Feedback and issues

Report an error, request a feature or ask the administrators a question. Account menu → **Feedback & issues** lists what you sent, its status and the administrators' replies.

## Sending

- On the **Feedback & issues** page, select **New report** (bug report) or **New question**. The type is one of **Bug report**, **Feature request** and **Question**.
- After you press 👎 on an answer, a **Report** button appears beside it. It opens the form with that answer and the artifact it made filled in as the **Pinned item**.
- You can add one screenshot (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, 5 MB or smaller).

## What is sent with it

- Always sent: app build, browser, screen size, language. When sent from a conversation, also the conversation type, the model used, the agent and process, stage status and failure reasons, and artifact version numbers.
- Only when you tick **Share this session with the administrators** are the conversation, the versions of the artifacts it made, the stage records and its files copied as they are at that moment. Later edits or deletion of the conversation do not change the copy.
- A conversation that ran on the internal-material route (strict-local) needs **I know this contains internal material and share it** ticked before the copy can be sent.

## Status

The status moves through **Received · Checking · Resolved · On hold**. When an administrator replies, **Feedback & issues** in the account menu shows the number of **New reply** items. An administrator opening the session copy is recorded in the audit log.
